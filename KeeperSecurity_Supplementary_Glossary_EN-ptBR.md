## How to use this file

Entries here are derived from approved translations already present in the Keeper Security TM. They represent consistent choices across multiple translation sessions and can be applied directly in new segments, subject to context. Where a note says **DNT**, keep the term in English. Where a note flags a preference (e.g. NOT 'indústria'), that preference overrides a naive dictionary choice.

Entries are alphabetical by English term. Acronyms are listed at the main entry; the short form is always retained in PT-BR unless stated otherwise.
