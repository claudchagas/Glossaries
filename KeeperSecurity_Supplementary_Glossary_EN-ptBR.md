## How to use this file

Entries here are derived from approved translations already present in the Keeper Security TM and from terminology locked during active Smartling review sessions. They represent consistent choices across multiple translation sessions and can be applied directly in new segments, subject to context. Where a note says **DNT**, keep the term in English. Where a note flags a preference (e.g. NOT 'indústria'), that preference overrides a naive dictionary choice.

Entries are alphabetical by English term. Acronyms are listed at the main entry; the short form is always retained in PT-BR unless stated otherwise.

Session locks added in Sep 2026 review session are marked **[SL]**.

|---|---|---|

| privilégio mínimo | menor privilégio | 'least privilege' — session-wide lock |
| deslocamento de privilégios | acúmulo de privilégios | 'privilege creep' — string 500 lock |
| deslocamento de permissões | acúmulo de permissões | 'permission creep' — parallel lock |
| privilégio de posição zero | privilégio permanente zero | 'zero standing privilege' — recurring source error |
